
# STREETLIGHTS

> **Run before the streetlights come on.**
>
> A 2D endless runner where an ordinary evening jog turns into a desperate fight for survival.

---

## About

Streetlights is a Unity-built endless runner inspired by classic arcade games, horror films, and the atmosphere of growing up with one simple rule:

**"Be home before the streetlights come on."**

You play as **Grace Jones**, a young woman simply trying to make it home after her evening run through Dallas. During the day everything feels normal. As time passes, the city changes. People become suspicious. Darkness falls. Then reality itself begins to unravel.

The familiar streets slowly transform into a nightmare filled with possessed civilians, monstrous creatures, and an otherworldly red miasma consuming everything in its path.

There is no destination.

Only survival.

---

## Story

Grace leaves for what should have been a routine run.

Her mother calls throughout the evening, checking on her and reminding her to get home before dark.

As the sun sets, strange things begin happening across Dallas.

Pedestrians stop acting normally.

The air becomes unnaturally quiet.

A crimson mist rolls through the streets.

One by one...

People begin changing.

Now Grace must outrun a city that no longer belongs to humanity.

---

## Features

- Endless side-scrolling gameplay
- Dynamic Day → Sunset → Night → Twisted Night progression
- Increasing difficulty based on survival time
- Random obstacle spawning
- Multiple enemy types
- Double Jump
- Slide mechanic
- Randomized dialogue from Grace's mother
- Dallas-inspired environments
- Original pixel art
- Atmospheric horror progression

---

## Gameplay Loop

The longer you survive...

- the game becomes faster
- enemies become more aggressive
- obstacle combinations become harder
- the world visually deteriorates
- the soundtrack becomes more intense
- Grace's mother's messages become increasingly desperate

Eventually...

Night falls.

Then something worse arrives.

---

## World Progression

### ☀ Day

Normal Dallas.

Pedestrians simply walk around the city.

Grace enjoys her run while chatting with her mother.

---

### 🌇 Sunset

Something feels...

off.

Suspicious people begin appearing.

The sky darkens.

Streetlights flicker to life.

---

### 🌙 Night

The city becomes dangerous.

Questionable strangers roam the streets.

Grace's mother repeatedly urges her to come home.

---

### 🔴 Twisted Night

Reality collapses.

A supernatural red miasma covers Dallas.

Citizens become possessed.

Monsters emerge.

The city itself feels alive.

Survive as long as you can.

## Why This Project Exists

Streetlights began as a simple Unity learning project.

As my programming skills improved, the project evolved into my first original indie game.

The goal is to create a horror experience where the player isn't trapped in a haunted house...

They're trapped in an entire city that changes around them.
